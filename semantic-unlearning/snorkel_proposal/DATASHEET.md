# Datasheet: SCALPEL v0.1

This datasheet follows Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets" (2021).

## Motivation

- **Purpose.** To measure *surgical* unlearning: removing one fact (or one entity) from a language model while keeping entangled knowledge. That means facts about the same subject, facts sharing the same answer, the same sentence, the same name tokens, and the ability to read the fact from context. The benchmark also tests whether the fact is gone from the weights or only hidden. Existing fact-unlearning benchmarks are saturated by a detect-then-edit method (see `EVIDENCE.md`) and do not test these properties.
- **Creators.** [PI / team, affiliation — to be filled in]. Built on this repository's unlearning research codebase.
- **Funding.** Proposed: Snorkel AI Open Benchmarks Grant (in-kind: expert data, compute, red-teaming).

## Composition

- **Instances.** Split files with *requests* (what a method is given), *probes* (what is asked), *allowed retain* text, and utility sets. Each probe has a role (forget/retain), a family (how it is asked, or what it controls for), a format (cloze / QA / chat), an answer plus aliases, and the request it belongs to. The full schema is in `scalpel/schema.py`.
- **Size.** Per split: 50 requests (configurable to 1k+). On Qwen2.5-0.5B, the pilot split has the probe counts shown in `results/pilot/*/split_summary.md`. Release target: 5 dev seeds and 5 hidden-test seeds per model, for 8 models from 5 families, 3B–27B (`ROADMAP.md`).
- **Sources.** MultiCounterFact, MQuAKE (original chains), zsRE (MEND split) and RWKU (entity track). After deduplication and conflict removal the pool holds 38,086 facts. Facts are merged on normalized (subject, relation, object).
- **Model-conditioned.** Every probe in a split is answered correctly by that split's base model under teacher forcing (the knowledge filter). Splits for different models contain different facts by design, and the scores remain comparable as "share of known knowledge".
- **Labels.** Answers come from the sources (Wikidata objects and aliases; MQuAKE answer aliases; zsRE answers; RWKU answers). Templates are author-written.
- **Missing information.** Families whose knowledge-filter yield is below 5 probes are reported but excluded from aggregates (`manifest.knowledge_filter_yield`).
- **Confidentiality.** All subjects are public figures or public entities present in the source datasets.
- **Partitions.** Subjects are split by salted hash into retain_train (10%), dev (45%) and test (45%). A subject appears in only one partition.

## Collection and preprocessing

- Source facts are loaded by `scalpel/sources`. If a cloze needs "the" before its object, " the" is appended (`needs_the`) so formatting does not decide the knowledge filter.
- (Subject, relation) pairs answered differently by two sources are dropped (186 in the pool). Facts whose subject and object contain each other are dropped (2,141).
- Probe families are generated by `scalpel/probes.py`. The knowledge filter and conflict removal are in `scalpel/build.py`.
- No crowdsourcing in v0.1. Planned expert data (ROADMAP M3): native-speaker verification of the cross-lingual templates; expert-written indirect references; a human-labelled calibration set for the generation grader.

## Uses

- **Intended use.** Evaluating unlearning, model-editing and guardrail methods under the declared tiers (behavioural / shipped weights / weights only).
- **Not intended for:** training models. A canary string is included, and contamination invalidates results. The benchmark also does not certify legal compliance with deletion requests: it measures behaviour and recoverability, and makes no claim of provable deletion.
- **Risks.** The probes contain facts about real public figures that are already public in the sources. The benchmark's purpose is to make removal measurable. The `in_context` notes contain counterfactual values and are clearly framed as notes.

## Distribution

- **Code:** Apache-2.0.
- **Dev split files:** CC BY 4.0, subject to the upstream terms in `DATA_LICENSE.md`. The zsRE terms must be confirmed before release.
- **Hidden test probes:** held by the maintainers. Evaluation is run by them on submitted checkpoints or method code.
- Hosting is planned on Hugging Face Datasets (dev splits), with a leaderboard space.

## Maintenance

- Versioned splits: the schema is `scalpel-split-v1`, and every result records the split fingerprint.
- Families that every leaderboard method scores above 98% on are flagged for replacement in the next version.
- Issues and contributions go through the repository. A new method plugs in through `--method plugin --method-arg target=module:Class`.
